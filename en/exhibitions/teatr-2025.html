In Two Hundred, Three Hundred Years · «Через двести, триста лет»

Barnaul · Altai Youth Theatre · 2025

Media artist for the project.

An installation-performance by Evgeny Malenchev, created with playwright Ekaterina Avgustenyak. It draws on stories by teenagers from Barnaul about the future of the city, nature, technology and relationships.

Visitors move through the theatre, encountering installations, performances and digital narratives. I contributed as the media artist. The composer was Andrey Platonov. The premiere took place on 18 September 2025 at the Valery Zolotukhin Festival.
