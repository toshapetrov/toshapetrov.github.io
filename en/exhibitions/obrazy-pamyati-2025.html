Images of Memory · «Образы памяти»

Kazan · MOÑ Theatre · 2025

Co-creator and media artist.

Images of Memory is a memorial media drama created as part of MEDIAMOÑ. I worked on it with director and playwright Ekaterina Avgustenyak and choreographer and dancer Nurbek Batulla.

On stage, a live performer encounters his own video diary, reinterpreted through neural networks. The project explores how movement, events and memories of a person are preserved, and what happens to these traces in a digital environment.
