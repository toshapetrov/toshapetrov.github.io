Two Banks of a River · «У реки два берега»

Suzdal · Collider Multimedia Platform, MIRA · 2026

Creator of evident / non-evident. Editing of the entire exhibition video programme and collaboration with the curator. Participation with the Berezovy Prompt art group.

Two Banks of a River was a joint project by MIRA and the Berezovy Prompt art group at the Collider multimedia platform in Suzdal. It opened on 21 March 2026 and explored dualities of perception: people and technology, the familiar and the new, the internal and the external.

The White Hall presented Backrooms by Ilay Dzhipov: an empty office that continues to function without people. In the Digital Hall, the artists’ video works explored different pairs of opposites.

My Role in the Exhibition

Alongside my own work, I worked closely with the curator and edited the entire exhibition video programme from the works of all the art group’s participants.

evident / non-evident

My work explored the pair evident / non-evident. It formed part of a collective video composition in which each artist approached duality through a personal theme and visual language.

The music for the video composition was composed by Dmitry Nevolnik.
