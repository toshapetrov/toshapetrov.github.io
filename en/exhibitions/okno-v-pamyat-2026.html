Window into Memory · «Окно в память»

Moscow · ZIL Cultural Centre · 4–15 November 2026

Creator of the video work In the Flow.

Window into Memory was a group exhibition of seven digital artists at the ZIL Cultural Centre. It explored a generation whose coming of age coincided with the digitisation of everyday life, and the devices through which we learned to see the world.

Display technologies were assigned to artists at random during preparation: slide projectors, old televisions, smartphones and other devices helped shape the works. The medium itself became part of the artistic statement.

In the Flow

My work is a looping video animation about endless information consumption. Characters with animal heads replace one another within a recursion of screens. A malfunction interrupts the flow, but everything then begins again.

Images and formats change, creating a sense of variety. Beneath that change, the same cycle repeats: overload, recovery, forgetting one’s exhaustion, and returning to familiar consumption.
