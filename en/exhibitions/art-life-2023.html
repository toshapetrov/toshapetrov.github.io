ARTLIFE FEST

Moscow · Manege Central Exhibition Hall · 11–17 November 2023

Digital animation of the painting Conversation.

For ARTLIFE FEST, I worked on a digital animation of the painting Conversation. The animation accompanied the physical painting and could be viewed on a smartphone screen.

The project credits use my former name, St. Laurent Jr.
