Seventh Dream (Museum Night) · «Седьмой сон» (Ночь музеев)»

Samara · Dom 77 Art Cluster · 16 May 2026

I took part in the media exhibition with the Berezovy Prompt art group.

Seventh Dream was a group media exhibition by the Berezovy Prompt art group, prepared with Gallery 419 for Museum Night in Samara. It was part of the programme at Dom 77 on 16 May 2026.

The exhibition explored fleeting impressions and memory. Seemingly minor events from the day became visual material: each artist offered a personal seventh dream, reflecting feelings and memories that usually remain at the edge of attention.
