Museums and Theatre

A Fashion Show in Costumes by Léon Bakst · «Дефиле в костюмах Льва Бакста»

2026 · Tretyakov Gallery, Moscow

A ten-minute video fashion show: twenty costumes from Bakst’s sketches become realistic garments in motion.

My role: costume visualisation and animation, art direction, script and editing. Music by Dima Nevolnik.

Images of Memory · «Образы памяти»

2025 · MOÑ Theatre, Kazan

A live performer encounters his video diary, reinterpreted through neural networks. Recorded movement becomes material for a conversation about memory.

My role: co-creator and media artist. Director and playwright: Ekaterina Avgustenyak. Performer: Nurbek Batulla.

About the project →

Narspi · «Нарспи»

2024 · Raduga Cultural and Exhibition Centre, Cheboksary

A multimedia exhibition based on Konstantin Ivanov’s poem, bringing together painting, poetry, music and digital technology.

My role: animating images with neural networks.
